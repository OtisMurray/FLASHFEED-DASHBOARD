# Focused Catalyst Return Validation

## Scope fixed before this run

This is a local, research-only follow-up. It does not alter the classifier,
rankings, entries, exits, thresholds, positions, or deployed dashboard.

The study reuses the 558 frozen position observations and structured catalyst
records generated by the preceding Catalyst Intelligence v2 study. Returns are
evaluated twice:

1. all frozen observations, including unresolved simulated marks;
2. finalized simulated positions only.

The second cohort is the more credible return check. The all-observation cohort
is retained only for comparison with the previous research.

## Fixed policy questions

1. **Baseline:** retain every frozen position.
2. **Any verified catalyst:** require a direct or market-wide structured event.
3. **Direct catalyst:** require a ticker-linked event.
4. **Aligned high confidence:** require a direct event with confidence at least
   0.85 whose direction agrees with the recorded AI direction.
5. **Reject capital structure:** exclude entries with a direct offering,
   dilution, warrant, conversion, or reverse-split event.
6. **Reject contradiction:** exclude entries with a high-confidence direct
   event whose direction contradicts the recorded AI direction.
7. **Direct aligned, no capital structure:** require a directionally aligned
   direct event with confidence at least 0.80 and no direct capital-structure
   event.

No policy parameter will be changed after seeing the output.

## Validation

- Development date: first available market date.
- Validation date: second available market date.
- Test date: third available market date.
- Added-cost stress: 0.25, 0.50, and 1.00 percentage points per selected trade.
- Remove best one and best three trades.
- Remove worst one and worst three trades.
- Deterministic day-block bootstrap.
- Report selection concentration by ticker and market date.
- Report an opportunity-adjusted return that assigns a zero return to skipped
  baseline opportunities.

## Evidence rule

A filter is not called return-improving unless it has at least 30 finalized
trades in both validation and test, positive mean selection delta on both,
positive opportunity-adjusted return after 0.50-point cost stress, a positive
mean after removing its best three trades, and a positive day-block bootstrap
lower bound. With only three stored dates, this standard is expected to remain
unmet; that is a data limitation, not permission to weaken the rule.
